Two minutes to set up.

The beta isn't signed by Apple or Microsoft yet, so you approve it once.

  1. Download: Apple Silicon (M1 and newer) or Intel.Not sure? Apple menu → About This Mac. "Chip: Apple M…" means Apple Silicon.
  2. Open the file and drag Open Flow into Applications.
  3. Open it once. macOS says it can't verify the app (I haven't paid Apple's $99/year fee yet): click Done, not Move to Trash, then System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway.Only the first time.
  4. Allow Microphone and Accessibility.Accessibility lets Open Flow type into other apps.
  5. Turn on AI cleanup: get a free key at console.groq.com and paste it on the AI cleanup page.
