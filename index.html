Two minutes to set up.

The beta isn't signed by Apple or Microsoft yet, so you approve it once.

  1. Download: Apple Silicon (M1 and newer) or Intel.Not sure? Apple menu → About This Mac. "Chip: Apple M…" means Apple Silicon.
  2. Open the file and drag Open Flow into Applications.
  3. Open it once. If macOS blocks it: System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway.Only the first time.
  4. Allow Microphone and Accessibility.Accessibility lets Open Flow type into other apps.
  5. Turn on AI cleanup: get a free key at console.groq.com and paste it on the AI cleanup page.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. Speech recognition runs on your computer. AI cleanup uses your own free Groq key.

What happens to my voice?

It's turned into text on your computer and never uploaded. With AI cleanup on, only the text goes to Groq. History stays on your computer for two weeks.

Why does my Mac say it can't check the app?

The beta isn't notarized yet. System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway, once.

It didn't type anything.

Check Microphone and Accessibility permission for Open Flow, then quit and reopen it.
