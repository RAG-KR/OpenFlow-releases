Install

Takes about two minutes. The beta isn't signed by Apple or Microsoft yet, so you approve it once.

  1. Download the Mac version: Apple Silicon (M1 and newer) or Intel.Not sure? Apple menu → About This Mac. "Chip: Apple M…" means Apple Silicon.
  2. Open the file and drag Open Flow into Applications.
  3. Open it once. macOS will block it. Go to System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway.You only do this once.
  4. Allow Microphone and Accessibility when asked.Accessibility lets Open Flow paste into other apps.
  5. Turn on AI cleanup: get a free key at console.groq.com and paste it on the AI cleanup page.Without it you still get dictation, just without the polish.
  1. Download the Windows installer.
  2. Run it. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click More info → Run anyway.You only do this once.
  3. Allow the microphone when asked.
  4. Turn on AI cleanup: get a free key at console.groq.com and paste it on the AI cleanup page.

Cleans up as you talk

Removes "um"s, fixes "3, no, 4", adds punctuation and lists. Never answers your questions; it just types them.

Learns your words

Fix a name after it's typed and Open Flow remembers it next time.

Rewrites in place

Select text and press a key to polish it, shorten it, or turn notes into a prompt. You see exactly what changed.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. Speech recognition runs on your computer. AI cleanup uses your own free Groq key.

What happens to my voice?

Your audio is turned into text on your computer and isn't uploaded. With AI cleanup on, only the text goes to Groq. History stays on your computer for two weeks.

Which languages?

English is best. Hindi works too (pick it under Settings → Dictation language). More languages are coming.

Why does my Mac say it can't check the app?

The beta isn't notarized by Apple yet. Use System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway, once.

It didn't type anything.

Check that Open Flow has Microphone and Accessibility permission (System Settings → Privacy & Security), then quit and reopen it.
